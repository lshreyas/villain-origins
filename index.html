Every era picks its enemies. Eight decades of Hollywood's primary antagonists — tagged by country of origin — constitute a moving record of who America has been afraid of. Nazis fade. Soviets crest twice, separated by an interregnum. The Middle East surges after September 2001. But the loudest, most persistent villain — from the 1970s on — has been America itself.

Method

Hand-curated sample of well-known Hollywood films, 1940s through early 2025. Each film's primary antagonist tagged by country of origin (or Non-national for aliens, AI, monsters, internal demons).

How to read

Exhibit A: hover or click streams to expand a dossier fragment of the films behind each ribbon. B: hover dots for film details. C: swap decades to see the threat geography. D: read top-to-bottom as a hierarchy. ESC resets the streamgraph.

Caveats

Iconic films, not exhaustive. Some characters defy a single flag (Le Chiffre, Keyser Söze, Loki) and are placed where most viewers locate them. Regional categories (Middle East, Latin America) collapse multiple national origins.
