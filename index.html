How to use

Hover the streamgraph to see each decade's villains. Click a stream (or pick a decade pill) to lock the view. Click again, or press ESC, to release.

Method

A hand-curated sample of well-known Hollywood films, 1940s–2020s. Each film's primary antagonist tagged by country of origin (or "Non-national" for aliens, AI, monsters, internal demons).

The story

Nazis fade. Soviets crest twice, separated by an interregnum. The Middle East surges after 9/11. But the loudest, most persistent villain—from the 1970s on—has been America itself.

Caveats

Iconic films, not exhaustive. Sample-driven. Some characters defy a single flag (Le Chiffre, Keyser Söze, Loki) and are placed where most viewers locate them.
